About

Yifei Zhang received her Ph.D. in Operations Research & Industrial Engineering from The Ohio State University in 2025. She is currently a Visiting Research Fellow there, working with Professor Theodore T. (Ted) Allen, at the intersection of optimization, machine learning, reinforcement learning, and computer vision.

IEEE Senior Member, Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers.

Research Interests

Nonlinear optimization, reinforcement learning, efficient global optimization for image processing and combinatorial decision-making problems, and LLMs for operations research.
